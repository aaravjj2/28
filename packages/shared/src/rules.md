# 28 Rules Contract

This file is a human-readable rules contract for Codex.

## Rank Strength

J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q > 8 > 7

## Points

J = 3
9 = 2
A = 1
10 = 1
K/Q/8/7 = 0

Total points = 28.

## Legal Play

1. Player must play only on their turn.
2. Player must play a card in their hand.
3. If player has led suit, player must follow led suit.
4. If player is void in led suit, player may play any card.
5. If hidden trump is played by a void player, trump becomes revealed.

## Trick Winner

1. If one or more trump cards are played, highest trump wins.
2. Otherwise highest card of led suit wins.
3. Trick winner leads next trick.
